# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A long-form reference technical documentation site for engineers and technical leaders who learn or operate AI infrastructure, data center networks, GPU systems, distributed training and inference, and MLOps. Users learn new concepts, compare system design trade-offs, and quickly find the evidence they need for real operations and performance analysis.

## Product Purpose

Connects learning notes and practical knowledge across AI data center systems to provide an accurate, navigable technical knowledge base. A successful experience clearly conveys complex system relationships, is readable anywhere in the GitHub documentation and the website, and lets users quickly reach the evidence they need through search, the knowledge graph, and visual materials.

## Brand Personality

Accuracy, calm, and expertise. Prioritizes verifiable explanations and technical judgment over exaggerated marketing language, and conveys complex content with restrained visual language.

## Anti-references

- Technical documentation that looks like a presentation deck with excessive color
- Screens that blur information with low-contrast lines, dotted lines, and auxiliary text
- UIs where decorative backgrounds and borders stand out more than the icons themselves
- Layouts that disrupt reading flow with unnecessary card nesting, excessive shadows, and decoration

## Design Principles

1. Technical meaning must be read before visual decoration.
2. Relationships such as hierarchy, connections, and warnings are distinguished not only by color but also by lines, shapes, and labels.
3. Maintain a calm density that does not disrupt the focus of users who read and navigate long documents.
4. Ensure core content and sources are understood identically on GitHub and the website.
5. Global styles must clearly separate content illustrations from UI glyphs to prevent unexpected inheritance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Targets WCAG 2.2 AA-level luminance contrast. In both light and dark themes, body text, icons, and diagram connectors must be clearly identifiable. Does not rely on color alone, respects keyboard focus and reduced-motion environments, and must display content mixing Korean and English stably.
